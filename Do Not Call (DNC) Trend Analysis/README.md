## Objective

Analyze Do Not Call (DNC) request trends to determine whether increased marketing efforts for promotional financing programs were contributing to customer opt-outs and identify the primary drivers behind DNC activity.

## Business Context

American First Finance offered promotional financing programs that were supported by outbound customer outreach and marketing efforts.

Leadership wanted visibility into whether DNC requests were increasing over time, what factors were contributing to the trend, and whether activity was being generated by automated systems or internal agents.

## Tools Used

* Excel
* Pivot Tables
* SQL
* Charts
* Trend Analysis

## Analysis Performed

* Month-over-month DNC trend analysis
* Marketing campaign impact analysis
* Agent-generated versus system-generated DNC activity
* Customer opt-out monitoring
* Operational performance reporting
* Root cause investigation

## Key Insights

* Identified trends in customer Do Not Call requests over time
* Measured the relationship between marketing activity and DNC volume
* Determined that a significant portion of DNC activity was agent-generated rather than system-generated
* Highlighted operational inconsistencies requiring additional employee training
* Improved visibility into customer communication preferences and compliance-related activity

## Business Impact

This analysis provided leadership with visibility into customer opt-out behavior and helped identify training opportunities that contributed to improved operational processes and compliance monitoring.
